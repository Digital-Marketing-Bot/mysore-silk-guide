Where to Buy Genuine Mysore Silk: KSIC Showrooms and Official Website

The safest way to buy a genuine Mysore silk saree is directly from Karnataka Silk Industries Corporation (KSIC), the registered proprietor of the Mysore Silk GI. This page points you to KSIC's own channels. We are not KSIC and we do not sell sarees.

Official KSIC showrooms

KSIC's website lists its showrooms by city. When we checked on 10 October 2026, the showroom pages on ksicsilk.com included these locations [9]:

  • Bengaluru: Jubilee Showroom (M.G. Road), Basaveshwara Nagar, K.G. Road, Gandhi Bazaar, Malleshwaram, Jayanagar, and KSIC Kalalokha at Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 2
  • Mysuru: Factory Showroom (Manandavadi Road), Zoo Road, K.R. Circle, J.L.B. Road and Kalidasa Road
  • Other cities: Channapatna, Hyderabad and Davanagere

For exact addresses, phone numbers and opening hours, open the Showrooms menu on the official KSIC website. Locations and timings can change, so check before you travel.

KSIC Mysore silk sarees online

KSIC's official website, www.ksicsilk.com, shows its saree collection with prices and price filters. If you want to look at a KSIC Mysore silk saree online, start there rather than with third-party listings, so that you are dealing with the source directly, and follow the ordering instructions given on that site.

Tips before you buy

  • Searching "KSIC showroom near me" on Google Maps is useful, but confirm the result against the list on ksicsilk.com.
  • Ask for the KSIC code and hologram on zari sarees, and keep your bill.
  • Be cautious of shops with similar-sounding names that do not appear on KSIC's list.

Contact KSIC

The Contact Us page on ksicsilk.com gives KSIC's head office details and email address. Use it for questions about stock, pricing or authenticity.
