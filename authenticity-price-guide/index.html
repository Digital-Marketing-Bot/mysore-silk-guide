How to Spot an Original Mysore Silk Saree, and What It Costs

Genuine Mysore silk is valuable, so imitations exist. These checks from official sources help you buy with confidence.

1. Look for KSIC's code and hologram

KSIC says it stamps a unique code number and a hologram on each of its zari sarees as identification marks [1]. Ask to see both, and keep your bill.

2. Understand the GI tag

"Mysore Silk" is a registered Geographical Indication (GI No. 11) with KSIC as the registered proprietor [1][4]. A saree sold as "Mysore silk" with no link to KSIC deserves extra questions.

3. Check the Silk Mark label

Silk Mark is a quality-assurance label run by the Silk Mark Organisation of India, an initiative of the Central Silk Board under the Ministry of Textiles, launched in 2004 [6]. It is affixed only to products made of 100% natural silk, and each label carries a hologram and a unique number that traces the product to an authorised user [6][7]. The Silk Mark website offers a Label Authentication option for checking that number. Remember: Silk Mark confirms pure silk, but on its own it does not prove a saree is Mysore silk.

4. The burn test (loose threads only)

KSIC suggests a simple test: take a small thread from both the warp and the weft and burn it. Pure silk smells like burning hair and leaves a black residue that crushes easily to powder [2]. Never damage the saree, and test plain silk threads, not zari.

Mysore silk saree price: what the official site shows

Price depends on design, weight and zari. When we checked KSIC's website on 10 October 2026, its women's saree listings ranged from ₹28,326 to ₹1,53,877, and its lowest price filter (₹0–15,000) showed no women's products [2][8]. Researching the KSIC Mysore silk saree starting price? Treat this as a dated snapshot: showroom stock and prices change, so confirm on www.ksicsilk.com or at a KSIC showroom.

Warning signs

Be wary of prices far below the official range, sellers who cannot show a code, hologram or bill, and "Mysore silk" used loosely for art silk or blends.
