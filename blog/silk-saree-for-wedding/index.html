Choosing a Silk Saree for a Wedding: A Practical Guide

A silk saree for a wedding is often a purchase you will keep for years, so it is worth choosing slowly. Whether you are the bride, a family member or a guest, these five questions will help.

1. How long will you wear it?

Wedding days are long. A heavy saree can look grand but tire you by evening. Mysore silk is known for being light and soft, so a Mysore silk saree for a wedding suits people who want comfort through hours of ceremonies, travel and photos.

2. What is your budget?

Set a range before you shop. Silk sarees with real zari are priced by design, weight and zari content. For Mysore silk, check the current price on KSIC's official website; our Authenticity & Price Guide records what the site showed on a given date.

3. Which colour and zari?

Traditional wedding colours such as red, maroon, green and gold are always safe, but pastels and jewel tones are popular for receptions. Look at the zari in daylight: a balanced border and pallu photograph well without overpowering you.

4. Is it genuine?

For Mysore silk, ask for KSIC's code number and hologram on zari sarees, and keep your bill [1]. For any silk saree, the Silk Mark label shows that the fabric is 100% natural silk [6]. Avoid deals that look too good to be true.

5. Have you planned the finishing?

Leave time for blouse stitching, fall and pico, and a trial drape. Our blouse design post has ideas that suit light silks.

After the wedding

Your saree can be worn again at festivals and family events. Store it clean and dry, wrap zari sarees in cotton cloth, and air them from time to time, as KSIC recommends [2].

Choosing well is less about following trends and more about comfort, honesty and fit. A genuine silk saree you love wearing will serve you for many celebrations.
